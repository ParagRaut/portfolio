## I ♥️ Odido

I've been working for Odido NL B.V. (formerly T-Mobile NL B.V.) since June 2016. My development work focuses on telecom-domain applications — customer-facing products like the self-service portal and web APIs for mobile apps, as well as internal tools for business and customer-care teams.

Along the way, I've also taken ownership of areas like SAST tooling administration and maintaining our CI/CD pipelines.

On the side, I've built a few applications that save manual time and effort, drawing on the functional and domain knowledge I've picked up here. This is also where I became much more comfortable with DevOps and agile (SAFe) ways of working.

Some of the projects I'm most proud of at Odido:
 - **1 Identity (IAM)** — aggregating all of a customer's subscriptions under a single account.
 - **Brand revamp** — merging the T-Mobile and Tele2 brands into the single Odido brand.

This is my current project, and I'm still learning something new every day.
